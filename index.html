Frequently asked

Is LOOP free?

Yes. You get 3 analyses per month for free, no signup needed. Pro plan ($29/mo) unlocks unlimited analyses, PDF export, and priority AI.

How accurate is the AI?

LOOP's AI is trained on real post-mortems from CB Insights, Failory, and regional databases. It's designed to challenge your assumptions, not to guarantee outcomes. Always validate with 50+ customer interviews.

Which regions are supported?

Currently: Bangladesh, India, Indonesia, Nigeria, Pakistan, and Global. More regions (Vietnam, Philippines, Egypt) coming soon.

Do you store my idea?

Analyses are stored locally in your browser for history. On Pro, they sync to your account. We never train AI on your ideas or share them.

What if I disagree with the verdict?

That's fine — LOOP is a tool, not a judge. Use the red flags and checklist as questions to validate, not absolute truth. Many successful startups had "high risk" ideas.
